## Competitive analysis

**Profitability** – Nvidia still enjoys a **gross margin of 71.1 %** and **operating margin of 60.4 %**, far ahead of AMD (49.5 % / 10.7 %) and Intel (34.8 % / -4.2 %). Analyst commentary (CNBC, May 2026) describes a “changing of the guard” as CPU demand for personal AI agents lifts Intel and AMD, while Nvidia “lags”.

## Conclusion
Nvidia remains the clear profitability leader, but its growth is increasingly dependent on large inventory builds and a diversified product push that adds margin and financing risk.  AMD shows the most attractive revenue growth among the non‑Nvidia peers and is benefiting from a resurgence in CPU demand for AI agents, yet it faces fierce competition, foundry‑capacity risk, and execution risk around acquisitions.  Intel is in a defensive position, with flat revenue, negative operating margins and a potentially massive impairment linked to its stalled 14A node.  For investors focused on pure AI‑GPU exposure, Nvidia’s scale and margin advantage still dominate, but the “changing of the guard” narrative and the emergence of CPU‑centric AI workloads suggest that AMD and Intel may capture a growing slice of the AI‑computing market.
